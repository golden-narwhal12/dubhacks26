# Parts and evidence

Existing Mega, ESP32-S3, Kingbright WP7113ID and Vishay MRS25 definitions/version references are retained. New examples explicitly choose generic passives. Version 1→2 migration adds board settings and telemetry mappings without replacing part identities or wiring.

- **Generic resistor:** 330 Ω initial editable assumption; no fixed voltage/direction, power or tolerance rating. Ω/kΩ/MΩ values are parsed and validated; mΩ means milli-ohms.
- **Generic LED:** red initial color; 1.9 V forward-voltage assumption and 10 mA analysis threshold are explicit editable assumptions, not manufacturer ratings. Color does not silently change forward voltage. Anode/cathode stay separate.
- **Potentiometer:** two ends/wiper, 10 kΩ editable end-to-end assumption. Taper, wiper setting and physical package order are unknown; no zero-ohm internal equivalence is asserted.
- **BusBoard BB830:** [Rev 7 datasheet](<https://www.busboard.com/documents/datasheets/BPS-DAT-(BB830)-Datasheet.pdf>), pp. 1–2, and [manufacturer FAQ](https://busboard.com/faq). 630 center holes: A–E and F–J per numbered row are separate five-hole groups. Four independent 50-hole rails run continuously through the midpoint. This is an exact BB830 model, not a claim about all breadboards. Hole IDs A1–J63 and LP/LN/RP/RN1–50 are stable app addresses. Explicit wires/attachments connect holes; proximity does not.
- **AS5600 module:** [supplied t026510 photo](evidence/as5600-t026510.png) shows left VCC/OUT/GND; right DIR/SCL/SDA/GPO. The listing says 3.3 V. Board schematic, regulator and pull-up routing remain unknown, and the photo label GPO is not proof of a general-purpose output. Chip reference [AS5600 DS000365](https://look.ams-osram.com/m/7059eac7531a86fd/original/AS5600-DS000365.pdf), indexed v1-06 20 Jun 2018, returned 404 on direct access during this follow-up. No exact breakout verification or 5 V tolerance is claimed. The official evaluation-board guide describes a different board and is not substituted for this module.
- **ITR9608 DIP-4:** User's HiLetgo listing names ITR9608/ITR9608F. Reference [Everlight ITR9608-F](https://everlightamericas.com/datasheets/ITR9608-F.pdf), DRX-0000076 Rev 7, released 20 Jul 2019, p. 6: 1 anode, 2 cathode, 3 collector, 4 emitter; p. 1: IR LED plus silicon phototransistor. The lot/suffix/authenticity remains provisional. Emitter current limiting and a detector load/pull-up are external circuit requirements. No comparator output, internal electrical short, or transferred absolute ratings are invented.
- **SG90:** Generic positional servo with logical supply/ground/control terminals. [TowerPro's manufacturer page](https://towerpro.com.tw/product/sg90-analog/) describes its own hardware and warns about counterfeits; it does not establish this inventory item's ratings or connector order. Supply/current, pulse limits and travel must be confirmed. No feedback wire or measured position is modeled.

New peripherals have no execution model. simavr executes Mega machine code, without simulating their electrical/optical/mechanical behavior. Debug can show them only when visible firmware actually transmits mapped fields. Readout association does not establish mechanical linkage or calibration.
